How to Use

  1. Paste your prompt, or enter the input tokens yourself.
  2. Set the reply length and how many requests you send each month.
  3. Compare the cost on every model, then copy the link or download a CSV.

What It Does

It counts the tokens in your prompt, adds the reply length you expect, and prices that one request on every model in our daily price list. It then multiplies by your monthly volume. You can filter by provider, search for a model, and download the table. Models whose context window cannot hold the prompt and reply are left out.

How Accurate Is It

  • Token counts for non-OpenAI models are estimates. Each provider counts tokens differently. The tool uses one count for every model, so costs for other providers can be off. OpenAI tokenizers give exact counts for OpenAI models. See why token counts differ between models.
  • Estimate mode is a guide, not an exact count. In our test of 32 sample texts it was off by about 8% on average against OpenAI's tokenizers, and 31 of the 32 fell inside the likely range the Token Counter shows. It was weakest on romanized Hindi. We did not test Claude or Gemini tokenizers, so for those models treat the count as approximate.
  • The reply length is your guess. Output usually costs more per token than input, so it matters. Change it to match your use.
  • List prices only. Caching, batch discounts, free tiers and negotiated rates are not included.

Is My Prompt Uploaded?

No. Counting and pricing happen in your browser. The share link stores only your settings, never your prompt text.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the cost calculated?

Input tokens times the input price, plus output tokens times the output price, each per million tokens. That is the cost of one request. The monthly figure multiplies it by your request count.

Why are some models missing?

Models whose context window is smaller than your prompt plus reply are hidden, and specialised models are hidden unless you tick the box. Cloud-reseller duplicates, embeddings and fine-tunes are not in the price list.

How is this different from the Token Counter?

The Token Counter shows the three cheapest models for sending your text once. This tool lists every model, includes the reply and your monthly volume, and lets you filter and download the table.

Where do prices come from?

From the open-source LiteLLM catalog, refreshed daily. They are not verified against provider billing, so confirm on the provider page before relying on a number.
