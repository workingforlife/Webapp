What This Estimates

It estimates the extra token bill for the hours when your traffic runs on a backup model instead of your main one. It spreads your monthly requests evenly across 730 hours, takes the share you choose for the outage window, and prices those requests on both models. The difference is the extra cost, or the saving if the backup is cheaper.

Not sure how long an outage to plan for? See the history of ChatGPT and Claude outages, with dates and durations.

What It Does Not Include

  • Lost revenue, support load and engineering time. Those are usually bigger than the token bill.
  • Failed requests. It assumes requests that fail on the main provider are not billed, and that you switch at once. Check your provider's billing terms.
  • Busy periods. Traffic is rarely even. An outage at your peak hour moves more requests than the average hour, so raise the traffic share or the hours to test it.
  • Rate limits and quotas. A backup account can have lower limits than your main one. See how to add a fallback to an LLM app.
  • Quality. A cheaper backup can answer worse. Test it on your own examples first.
  • Discounts and contracts. It uses list prices per million tokens.

How to Use It

  • Pick your main model and a backup, ideally from a different provider.
  • Enter a typical request and your monthly volume, then the length of an outage you want to plan for.
  • Look at the extra cost per outage and per year, then compare other backups in the table.

Questions

Will a failover double my costs?

Not for the whole month. It only affects the requests moved during the outage. The result shows the extra cost as a share of your normal monthly bill.

Does a standby model cost anything while it is idle?

With most pay-as-you-go APIs, no. You pay for tokens used. Check your provider for minimum fees or reserved capacity.

Where do the prices come from?

From the open-source LiteLLM catalog, refreshed daily. They are not verified against provider billing, so confirm on the provider page before relying on a number.
