What It Does

Paste an old and a new version of a prompt and Prompt Diff marks what was removed and what was added. It counts the tokens in both versions, shows the change for each edit, and turns the difference into a cost per request and per month on the model you choose.

How the Numbers Work

  • Total change. The tokens in the whole revised prompt minus the whole original. This is the number to trust.
  • Tokens per edit. Each change is counted on its removed text and its added text separately. A tokenizer's counts depend on the neighbouring text, so these can differ slightly from the total.
  • Estimate or exact. The estimate works for any model and the Token Counter shows its likely range. Choose an exact OpenAI tokenizer for OpenAI models. Other providers can count differently, see why token counts differ.
  • Cost. Tokens times the model's input price, from the daily price list, times your monthly requests.

What It Does Not Tell You

Fewer tokens is cheaper, not better. A shorter prompt can drop an instruction the model needed, so compare the answers as well. See how to test a prompt change before shipping. To find savings in the first place, try the Prompt Token Optimizer.

Questions

Is my prompt uploaded?

No. The comparison runs in your browser. The page fetches the price list, and the exact tokenizer files if you choose one, but your text is not part of those requests. There is no share link on purpose, because it would put your prompts in the address.

Why do the edits not add up to the total?

Each edit is counted on its own, but tokens depend on the text around them. Words at the edge of an edit can join their neighbours into different tokens. Use the total change for decisions.

Do spaces and line breaks count?

Yes, they can cost tokens, and a change that only adds or removes whitespace is shown with dots and line-break marks. Hidden characters are covered in how to save tokens in a prompt.

Should I compare by words or by lines?

Words shows exactly what changed inside a sentence. Lines is easier to read for lists and templates where whole lines were rewritten. Very long prompts switch to lines on their own.

Where do the prices come from?

From the open-source LiteLLM catalog, refreshed daily. They are not verified against provider billing, so confirm on the provider page before relying on a number.
