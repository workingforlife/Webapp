Deprecation and Deadline Checker

Paste your code, config or a list of model names. See which promo prices, retirements and removals affect you, how many days are left, and what each change does to your monthly bill.

Deadline list last reviewed 25 November 2026. The monthly figures use your numbers and the list prices shown. They are illustrations, not a quote.

What It Is For

Providers remove models, end promotional prices and retire features on fixed dates, often with only a few weeks of notice. A removed feature returns an error, and an ended promotion raises your bill without any change on your side. This tool keeps a short, dated list of those events. You paste the model names you use, and it shows only the ones that apply to you.

How to Read the Results

  • Checked on the provider page means we read the date and the price on the provider's own documentation, and the source link is shown.
  • Reported, not checked means the date or price comes from a third-party site only. Confirm it on the provider page before you act.
  • The monthly figure compares the price you pay now with the option listed, using the token volumes you enter. A promotion's price after it ends is often not published in advance. In that case the tool says so and compares against the named alternative instead.

What It Does Not Do

  • It does not read your account or your bill. It only matches text you paste against its list.
  • It does not cover every model or every provider. The list is short on purpose, and each entry is checked by hand. See the Price Change Tracker for daily list-price movements across many models.
  • It cannot tell whether a replacement model is good enough for your task. Test it on your own prompts before you switch.

Where To Go Next

Compare replacements in the Model Picker, look up exact prices in Model Prices, or rebuild your forecast in the Cost Forecaster. For the background, read the 30 November checklist.

Questions

Is my code uploaded?

No. The matching runs in your browser and the page does not send your text anywhere.

How often is the list updated?

We review it by hand when a provider announces a change. The review date is shown above the results. If a provider changes a date, trust the provider page over this list.

Why does a model show "applies only if you use fast mode"?

Some removals affect one feature, not the whole model. If the model name is present but the feature is not, your calls should keep working. Check your request settings to be sure.

What if my model is not listed?

Then nothing on this list affects it. That does not mean no change is coming. Check the provider's deprecation page for your model.
